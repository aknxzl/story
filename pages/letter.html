Love Letter

To the love of my life,

Hi baby, we made it, it’s our first anniversary! It’s crazy to think we’ve made it through 12 whole monthsaries together. Confessing to you on the 1st of October was the moment our relationship grew into something more. We grew closer, we showed each other how much we truly cared, and most importantly, we showed each other how much we loved each other. I remember when you were complaining about the song “We Fell in Love in October,” and the moment you told me about it, I knew I had to confess my love to you in October hehehe. Even though you told me to wait until December or January, I’m so glad you got impatient and started dating me on the 11th :DDDD.

I love the way we bond over games, especially the Roblox games we usually play together. Watching you play, minding your own business and looking so happy and carefree, still makes me cry every time I think back to those moments. Our random conversations that switch topics every single time are some of my favourite memories of this relationship. I love how you suddenly bring up something completely new topic in the middle of our conversation, and I love seeing how much joy you get from talking to me. I can just imagine the gleeful expression you have during those times. Honestly, just seeing you happy in general makes me stop whatever I’m doing and just admire you while you’re going at it.

Remember the first movie we watched after we got together? It was Mulan, and I will always treasure that movie night. Right after that came the series we watched and bonded over, TSITP (The Summer I Turned Pretty). Honestly, I thought it was going to be boring and that we’d watch a couple of episodes and never finish it. But it was different. Not only was it entertaining, but watching it with you made it so much more memorable. The best part of all those TSITP memories is simply knowing that I was watching it with you.

Eating with you is something I enjoy every single time. Remember the first time we had Burger King together? That was the moment I realised eating with you was completely different from eating on my own. The food tasted so much better, and I was happier while eating. Eating with you also taught me to slow down and savour my food. You taught me how to actually enjoy my food just by eating with me. That’s also one of the reasons I’ve started buying you food regularly, because I really do love eating with you. It’s something we can always bond over, since we both love food :))).

Now that my mom is in contact with you, I hope she sees what a wonderful and lovely person you truly are. I’m sure she already knows you’re a sweet girl, and as time goes by, I know she’ll understand why I fell in love with you.

To end this letter, I want to thank you for choosing me this past year. Not only have have gone through so much together, we were also there for each other during big moments in our lives. We pushed through many challenges, and we’re still together, standing strong and loving each other more and more. I pray that we make it through another 12 monthsaries, just like we already have. Thank you for being my favourite person, my best friend, and my lover. Know that I love you with all my heart, and if I could go back to the moment I confessed to you, I would choose to confess my love to you every single time.

I love you,
Wee Chan
